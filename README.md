# Phantom Blade Zero Wiki

Complete community wiki for **Phantom Blade Zero** (影之刃零) — the dark wuxia action RPG from S-GAME, released October 29, 2026 on PS5 and PC.

Live site: **https://phantomblade.anydaykit.com**

## What's here

- **Guides** — getting started, combat mechanics, progression and build advice for Soul's 66-day journey
- **Bosses** — attack patterns, phases, and strategies for the martial artists and monstrosities of the Phantom World
- **Weapons** — the 30+ weapon styles of the game, their movesets, and how to master each
- **Items** — accessories, upgrade materials, and where to find them

Every article carries a last-verified date. Facts are sourced from official announcements and first-hand reporting — nothing is fabricated.

## About the game

- **Developer / Publisher:** S-GAME (灵游坊)
- **Platforms:** PS5, PC (Steam, Epic Games Store, WeGame, TapTap)
- **Genre:** Action RPG ("kung fu punk")
- **Engine:** Unreal Engine 5
- **Protagonist:** Soul (魂), a swordsman with 66 days left to live
