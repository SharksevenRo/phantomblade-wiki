## How do accessories work?

Accessories are passive items socketed into limited slots:

- **2 slots by default, expandable up to 6** via abilities (Boundless Capacity is the early pickup)
- Three types — **attack, defense, support** — with a maximum of **3 per type** socketed at once

The Cornucopia pre-order accessory, for reference, sits in the support family and boosts Remnant Dust drops by 10% — and like everything pre-order, it's earnable in-game (see the [editions guide](/guides/editions-guide/)).

## What is Remnant Dust for?

**Remnant Dust (意识尘埃)** is the economy's single currency: it buys [ability training](/guides/abilities-progression/), funds [weapon](/weapons/getting-started/) and Phantom Edge upgrades, and pays shop prices. It is *not* experience — there's no leveling curve to grind, and since enemies stay dead when you rest, dust arrives through exploration rather than repetition. Spend it; the world keeps offering more.

## Which materials should I hunt?

| Material | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Black Stone** (4 fragments) | Permanent +10% attack & guard break; unlocks ability-row nodes |
| **Sha-Chi Amber** (4 fragments) | Permanent +10% maximum Sha-Chi |
| **Memory Blossom** (petal sets) | Permanent max-health boosts at roughly 5% / 7% / 10% tiers |
| **Banyan Heart** | Late-game advanced material; about 8 exist per playthrough and Quick Hand Zhang sells them for 100,000 dust each |

Fragments are the quiet engine of a build: four Black Stone fragments is a permanent damage tier no accessory can match, and more max Sha-Chi means longer combo strings before you need the weapon-swap refill.

<Callout type="tip">Priorities when a region opens: bell first, fragments second, chests third. The [beginner's guide](/guides/getting-started/) covers the route logic, and bosses check your permanent stats more than your shopping — spend early, explore wide.</Callout>
