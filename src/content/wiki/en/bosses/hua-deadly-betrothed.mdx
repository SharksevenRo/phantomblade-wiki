## Who is Hua, Deadly Betrothed?

**Hua, Deadly Betrothed** is the standout optional duel of **Weeping Creek Valley** — a ghostly bride in red whose fight mixes fast blade strings with oppressive grab pressure. She drops the **Scarlet Wraithblade**, and she is also the fight the game's first patch singled out: **patch 1.0302256 (November 4, 2026) reduced the frequency of her grab attacks on Wayfarer and Pathbreaker** difficulties (see the [update history](/guides/update-history/)). Hellwalker and 66 Days were left untouched — on those, expect the original aggression.

![A ghostly bride in red wields a curved blade among white lanterns](/images/articles/pbz-hua-betrothed.jpg)

## What is her move set?

- **Blade strings with blue flashes** — parry these. Her multi-hit flourishes are fast but rhythmic; once you catch the beat, her Guard Break bar fills quickly.
- **Red-flash grabs** — dodge, never parry. Pre-patch these were relentless on every difficulty; post-patch they are noticeably sparser below Hellwalker.
- **Veil-closing teleport** — she vanishes in a flutter of red and reappears at melee range, usually into a string opener. The reappearance has an audio cue; hold your guard reaction until you see the flash color.
- **Phase-two wraith dance** — below half health she chains longer combos and mixes grabs into strings. This is where fights are won or lost.

## How do I beat her?

1. **Learn the two-color rhythm first.** Enter the fight intending to survive, not to win — map which attacks flash blue and which flash red. Her string structure is fixed enough that ten minutes of reads pays off for the rest of the game.
2. **Save dodges for red only.** Panic-dodging blue attacks burns your positioning and gets you clipped by the string's real finisher. Parry the blues; that is also how you break her.
3. **Punish the teleport.** After she reappears, her first attack is heavily telegraphed. Whiff-punish it, then disengage — do not extend into her second layer.
4. **Burst phase two.** Use your Phantom Edges when she starts the wraith dance. A Destructive Edge interrupt (like the [Dread Axe](/bosses/peng-meng/)) cuts her longest combo short.

<Callout type="tip">Extra Medicine charges are the single best investment for this duel — train **Healing Art** (5,000 Dust) and bring a full pouch. On Wayfarer and Pathbreaker, the November 4 patch already thins her grabs; on Hellwalker, every charge counts double.</Callout>

## What are the rewards?

| Reward | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Scarlet Wraithblade** | A fast main weapon with bleed-flavored flavor — strong pairing with the [weapon upgrade economy](/weapons/weapon-upgrades/) refund rules |
| Heavy Remnant Dust payout | Optional bosses pay far above story fights |
| Trophy progress | Counts toward the boss-slayer trophies on the road to platinum |

## Should I fight her now or later?

She is **beatable the moment you meet her**, but she does not gate anything. If her phase two keeps ending your run, clear more of the valley first — the permanent collectibles you gather (see the [collectibles guide](/guides/upgrade-collectibles/)) are the intended answer to every wall in this game. Come back with two more Blackstones and the fight changes character.
