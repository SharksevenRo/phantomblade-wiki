## How are bosses structured?

Phantom Blade Zero has **60+ boss fights** across **nine regions**. Most main-story bosses drop a **main weapon** or progress item; optional duels add **Phantom Edges**, **Acolyte Masks**, and accessory upgrades on top. A few fights recur — The Hunt and You Loong each return in harder forms, and the Gold-Masked One waits inside all five **Long Pillar** trials.

Use this page as the roadmap; detailed strategies live in the individual boss guides (for example [The Hunt](/bosses/the-hunt/), [Peng Meng](/bosses/peng-meng/), and [Hua, Deadly Betrothed](/bosses/hua-deadly-betrothed/)).

## Which fights matter most?

- **True ending gates:** the four Acolyte Masks, Blizzard Blademaster's mirror realm, Zhu Yuan, Bi Feng, the five Gold-Masked One pillars — consolidated in the [endings guide](/guides/endings-guide/).
- **Exploration keys:** Peng Meng (chains), The Hunt (grapple), Wan Jun (floorboards), Huangxing (dials), Lei Dang (walls) — every one multiplies what the [map checklist](/guides/map-completion/) can clear.
- **Trophy farming:** the boss-slayer trophies tick automatically as you work this list in order; the only missable side is expiring tales that hide fights (see [expiring side quests](/guides/expiring-side-quests/)).

<Callout type="tip">Bosses do not respawn and there is no rematch arena at launch — if you love a fight, savor it. For build checks between fights, keep the [weapon upgrade economy](/weapons/weapon-upgrades/) refund rules in mind: free below Level 10 means every boss drop is a test-drive, not a commitment.</Callout>
