## How many bosses are there?

S-GAME's official line is **more than 50 bosses** across the journey — a mix of martial-artist duels, monsters, and set-piece encounters. The pre-release showcases gave a taste of the range: a **lion-dance boss** that breathes fire from its ceremonial head, and a **drunken swordsman** whose blade work weaves wine and killing intent.

![A massive lion-dance boss breathes fire across a festival square at night](/images/articles/pbz-bosses.jpg)

## How do boss fights work?

Bosses are **phase-based**, and the color language from the [combat system](/guides/combat-system/) applies at full intensity: blue techniques want a parry, red techniques demand a dodge, and dark techniques drain your Sha-Chi. Two rules make the fights learner-friendly:

- **Phase checkpoints.** Die in a boss's second phase and you restart at that phase — the game makes you practice the part that's killing you, not the part you've already solved.
- **Generous respawns.** You return to the last bell with enemies you've already beaten still dead, so the run back is short.

Executions and Power Surges still grant invulnerability, and an execution **refills your Sha-Chi** — spending your resources aggressively is usually correct, not reckless.

## Which bosses matter most for completion?

Two optional bosses — **Zhu Yuan and Bi Feng** — are required alongside full side-quest completion for the [true ending](/guides/length-and-endings/). They're the bosses to prioritize before pushing the story past its point of no return, alongside every **purple-icon side quest**, since those expire.

<Callout type="tip">Standard duel recipe: learn the first phase's color tells, save Phantom Edge charges for phase transitions, and remember the weapon swap refills your Sha-Chi — the [weapon system](/weapons/getting-started/) is your mid-fight battery. For the aftermath, see [New Cycle](/guides/new-cycle/) for NG+ scaling.</Callout>
