## Who is The Hunt?

**The Hunt, Hunter of the Order**, is the first true boss of Phantom Blade Zero — your exam on everything the tutorial taught. You meet him twice in **Weeping Creek Valley**: a first encounter in the *Reckless Pursuit* chapter, and a harder rematch during *Steel and Steam*. The second fight rewards the **Hunter's Chain Sickle**, a Martial Phantom Edge that also serves as a grapple tool for distant points (see the [Phantom Edges locations](/weapons/phantom-edge-locations/) guide for what else the valley hides).

![A masked hunter brandishes a chain sickle in a misty creek valley](/images/articles/pbz-the-hunt.jpg)

## What is The Hunt's move set?

Both encounters run on the same grammar, with the rematch adding speed and combo length:

- **Blue-flash attacks** — parry these. His sickle swings have generous parry windows on Wayfarer and Pathbreaker, and successful parries build toward Guard Break.
- **Red-flash attacks** — dodge these. The Hunt's lunging grab and spinning flourish are unparryable; dodge *through* him, not away, to stay in range for the punish.
- **Chain pulls** — at mid range he reels himself in or drags the sickle back across the arena. Sidestep and treat the recovery as your damage window.

If the flash-color system is still new to you, read the [combat system guide](/guides/combat-system/) first — this fight assumes it.

## How do I beat the first encounter?

1. **Stay close.** The Hunt is most dangerous at mid range where his chain moves connect. Pressure him up close and his options shrink to melee strings you can read.
2. **Parry the opener, dodge the finisher.** Most strings end with a red-flash flourish — dodge that, then counter-attack the recovery.
3. **Break his guard.** Parries fill his posture bar fast. When he staggers, execute for heavy damage and a Remnant Spark refund with the right ability trained (see [best early abilities](/guides/best-abilities-first/)).
4. **Use the arena.** The first arena has room to disengage and heal; do not heal within his chain's reach.

<Callout type="tip">Train **Steady Composure** (5,000 Remnant Dust) before this fight — its stronger post-dodge interrupts turn his long recoveries into guaranteed counter windows.</Callout>

## What changes in the second encounter?

The *Steel and Steam* rematch adds pressure rather than new tricks:

- **Faster strings, less recovery.** Your punish windows shrink — dodge later, attack sooner.
- **More chain coverage.** He uses the sickle's pull to close gaps you used to be safe in. Keep moving along the arena's edges.
- **Phase two aggression.** Below half health he chains red-flash lunges back to back. Save your Medicine for this stretch, and consider a defensive accessory from the [items overview](/items/getting-started/).

## What are the rewards?

| Encounter | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| First (Reckless Pursuit) | Progression, Remnant Dust |
| Second (Steel and Steam) | **Hunter's Chain Sickle** (Martial Phantom Edge — combat + grapple traversal) |

<Callout type="info">The Chain Sickle's grapple opens shortcut points and chest ledges across every region. After the fight, backtrack through Weeping Creek Valley with the [map completion checklist](/guides/map-completion/) — several collectibles only open up with your new Edge.</Callout>
