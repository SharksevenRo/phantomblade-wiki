## What are Banyan Tree challenges?

Banyan Trees are **self-contained combat trials** hidden across Phantom Blade Zero's nine regions. Interact with one and you face escalating waves of enemies that culminate in **duo boss fights** — two named martial artists attacking you at once. Each tree rewards exactly **one Banyan's Heart**, and there are **eight hearts (and eight trees) per playthrough**.

Those hearts feed the most powerful nodes on the ability tree, which is why these fights are the single most valuable optional content in the game — see the [collectibles guide](/guides/upgrade-collectibles/) for what each heart unlocks.

![A warrior faces two duelists beneath a giant glowing banyan tree](/images/articles/pbz-banyan.jpg)

## When do Banyan Trees unlock?

The heart-gated ability rows appear in the **latter half of the campaign**, when the game gives you the quest pointing toward the forest where the trees wait. Before that, exploration still pays — fragments, chests, and weapons — but the trees themselves are a mid-to-late-game spike. If you are still learning the fundamentals, the [boss basics guide](/bosses/getting-started/) covers the phase structure and counter windows these trials test hard.

## How do duo boss fights work?

Two bosses share the arena, and the trial is about **controlling the tempo**, not trading hits with both:

- **Never fight both at once.** Stay mobile, let one commit to a combo, and punish the recovery while the second closes distance.
- **Red-flash attacks cannot be parried** — dodge them. **Blue-flash attacks cannot be dodged safely** — parry them. The two-color system is the core of every high-end fight; the full breakdown is in the [combat system guide](/guides/combat-system/).
- **Finishing one boss first is the win condition.** Focus damage on whichever opponent overextends; the survivor alone is a standard duel.
- **Guard Break is your opener.** Breaking an enemy's stance sets up an execution, and executions restore resources with the right ability trained — the [best early abilities](/guides/best-abilities-first/) include exactly those sustain picks.

<Callout type="tip">Restock before each tree: train abilities, upgrade weapons at the Bell (refunds are free up to Level 10 — see the [weapon upgrade guide](/weapons/weapon-upgrades/)), and slot boss-specific accessories. You only get one heart per tree per playthrough; walk in prepared.</Callout>

## What if I miss a heart?

- **Quick Hand Zhang** sells a small number of hearts for **100,000 Remnant Dust each** — a very expensive fallback, since the same currency funds your whole ability tree.
- **Next cycle:** any heart you missed returns on the next playthrough. Cycles are covered in [New Cycle explained](/guides/new-cycle/).
- The advanced abilities need one heart each and there are exactly eight — so a full clear of all eight trees, plus Zhang's stock, is how you max the inter-row perks without waiting for NG+.

## Reward checklist

- 8 Banyan's Hearts per playthrough (one per tree)
- Heavy Remnant Dust payouts from the waves themselves
- Progress toward map completion percentages
- Access to the ability tree's strongest row — plan your heart order in the [collectibles guide](/guides/upgrade-collectibles/)

<Callout type="warn">The trees are some of the hardest encounters in the game — harder than most story bosses. If a tree walls you, clear more of the campaign first: the permanent stat collectibles you gather along the way (Blackstones, Sha-Chi Ambers, Memory Flowers) are the intended answer.</Callout>
