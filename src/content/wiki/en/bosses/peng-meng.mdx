## Who is Peng Meng?

**Peng Meng, the Axemaster**, guards the *Against the Flow* chapter in **Weeping Creek Valley**. He is the game's first heavyweight: a brute who trades speed for massive, armor-plated swings. He drops the **Dread Axe**, a Destructive Phantom Edge — and the Dread Axe is also a key: it breaks the **iron chain barriers** blocking side paths across the valley and beyond.

![A massive axemaster raises his greataxe on a ruined battlefield](/images/articles/pbz-peng-meng.jpg)

## What is Peng Meng's move set?

- **Overhead slam** — his opener. A long wind-up with a red-flash finish. Dodge sideways at the last beat; the axe sticks in the ground for a generous punish window.
- **Sweeping arcs** — wide horizontal swings, usually in pairs. The first is often blue-flash (parry it), the second red-flash (dodge it).
- **Charging grab** — he lowers his shoulder and rushes. Red flash. Dodge through the charge and he runs past you into a wall stun — your biggest damage window of the fight.
- **Armor stance** — mid-fight he plants his feet and gains hyper-armor on light hits. Stop attacking into it and build his posture bar with parries instead.

## How do I beat him?

1. **Dodge late, not early.** Every swing is slow enough that panicking early gets you clipped by the tracking. Wait for the flash, then move.
2. **Parry the blue, dodge the red.** Two-color discipline is the whole fight. Parries fill his Guard Break bar fast because his attacks are heavy.
3. **Punish the recovery, not the swing.** His axe stays planted after slams. One or two heavy attacks, then reset — greedy third hits get you caught by the follow-up.
4. **Execute the stagger.** Guard Break him and the execution does huge damage. The [boss fundamentals](/bosses/getting-started/) guide covers the posture loop in detail.

<Callout type="warn">Do not spam light attacks during his armor stance — he will out-trade you. Switch to parries until the stance drops, then resume pressure.</Callout>

## What do I get for winning?

| Reward | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Dread Axe** (Destructive Phantom Edge) | Heavy combat armament **and** the tool that breaks chain barriers — red obstacle icons on your map turn green |
| Progress toward region completion | The barriers it opens hide chests, fragments, and side paths |

<Callout type="tip">After the fight, immediately backtrack: the chain barriers you passed on the way in are now breakable. Sweep the valley with the [map completion checklist](/guides/map-completion/) before moving to Crimson Forest — enemies do not respawn, so nothing you clear comes back.</Callout>

## How does Peng Meng compare to The Hunt?

If you fought [The Hunt](/bosses/the-hunt/) first, Peng Meng is the opposite exam: The Hunt tests reaction speed with fast strings; Peng Meng tests patience with slow, punishing reads. Players who struggled with The Hunt often find Peng Meng easier — every attack has a long, readable wind-up.
