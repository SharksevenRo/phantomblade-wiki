## How should you read this tier list?

Phantom Blade Zero has **26 main weapons across three classes** — one-handed, dual-wield, two-handed — and no weapon is carried by stats alone: every weapon's power depends on your ability build, your accessories, and whether you enjoy its moveset. Two design facts make this list different from most tier lists:

- **Refunds are free up to Level 10** (see the [upgrade economy guide](/weapons/weapon-upgrades/)), so "best" is a cheap experiment, not a commitment.
- **Patch 1.0302256 (November 4, 2026)** updated weapon descriptions and tuned combat behavior — treat exact rankings as snapshot opinions, not physics.

Use this list as a shortlist of what to try next, not a verdict on what you must use. Every weapon below comes from a boss or quest you will encounter naturally — see the [boss order and rewards list](/bosses/boss-order/) for the full mapping.

## Early game (Weeping Creek Valley, Crimson Forest)

| Weapon | Source | Why it stands out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Jagged Steel** | Boss drop (Jagged Steel) | The early-game monster: a bleed-flavored build with cheap, high-impact upgrade thresholds — the standard recommendation for your first ten hours |
| **Outlaw Warblade** | Boss drop (Jing Ye) | Life-drain flavor that sustains long fights — with the caveat that puppets and golden bodies ignore the drain |
| **Fanatic's Broken Blade** | Boss drop (Hua Quanxiao) | A "drunken sword" gimmick — a tipsy state changes attacks into flying-sword techniques while restoring Sha-Chi; high ceiling, steeper learning curve |
| **Scarlet Wraithblade** | Optional boss ([Hua, Deadly Betrothed](/bosses/hua-deadly-betrothed/)) | Fast blade with strong fundamentals — the earliest optional-weapon check worth detouring for |

## How do I pick my weapon pair?

You carry two weapons and swap constantly — swap-refill is a core mechanic (see the [combat system guide](/guides/combat-system/)). A reliable pairing formula:

1. **One "comfort" weapon** whose strings you can execute sleepily — this is your 80% weapon.
2. **One "tool" weapon** that covers the comfort weapon's gap: a slow heavy hitter paired with a fast dueling blade, or a defensive auto-counter paired with an aggression monster.
3. **Match edges to build.** Your two [Phantom Edges](/weapons/phantom-edge-locations/) are where burst and utility live — pick weapons that fill while the Edge does the killing.

<Callout type="tip">The honest top of the tier list is "whatever you have upgraded." A Level 15 comfortable weapon beats a Level 3 theoretical S-tier every time — and since refunds are free below Level 10, level the thing you like and revisit the shortlist when a boss drop tempts you.</Callout>

## Why isn't there a single "best weapon"?

Because the game's difficulty lives in execution, not spreadsheets. Bosses telegraph in two colors; your weapon determines what you can do inside those windows, and different players hit different windows. The launch-week consensus clusters around the standouts above — but since every weapon drops from content you were going to clear anyway, the practical advice is: upgrade what drops, refund what disappoints, and let your hands pick the tier list.
