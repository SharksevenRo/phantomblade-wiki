## What weapon classes are there?

Soul carries **two main weapons** at once, freely swapped mid-combo, drawn from **30+ weapons** in three broad classes:

| Class | Character |
| --- | --- |
| **One-handed** | Balanced speed and power; the all-rounder profile |
| **Two-handed** | Slow, heavy hits with strong guard-break pressure |
| **Dual-wield** | Fast, long chains; weaker individual hits, relentless pressure |

Every weapon has its own normal combos, Sha-Chi combos, unique traits, and a signature **Power Surge** technique.

![A wall of ornate Chinese blades hangs in a forge lit by coals](/images/articles/pbz-weapons.jpg)

## Why is swapping weapons the key mechanic?

Because **swapping instantly refills your Sha-Chi** — on a cooldown of roughly five seconds, shortenable through skills. Offense drains the bar, the swap restores it, and the change of moveset keeps pressure on. Playing one weapon only is leaving your best resource tool unused; pairing a heavy breaker with a fast chain weapon covers both sides of the [combat system](/guides/combat-system/).

## How do upgrades work?

- Weapon upgrades raise damage, status, and guard break, and **unlock new skills at key thresholds (5 / 10 / 15 / 25)**.
- **Reforge** rolls a weapon back and returns your materials — free early on, later costing an item per level refunded. Experimentation is cheap.
- **Soul Temper** (unlocked by talking to Myst) gives each weapon a bespoke enhancement with unique materials — and a few weapons change into entirely new forms, with altered effects. Treat those transformations as hidden upgrades worth hunting materials for.

<Callout type="tip">Upgrade breadth beats depth early: two viable weapons enable the refill loop, while a single maxed blade can't. Materials feed from the same exploration economy as everything else — see [items & resources](/items/getting-started/), and pair your blades with the right [Phantom Edges](/weapons/phantom-edges/) for phase damage.</Callout>
