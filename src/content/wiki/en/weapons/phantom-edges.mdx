## What are Phantom Edges?

**Phantom Edges (影之武装)** are supernatural techniques Soul collects across the world — the game's second arsenal alongside his [main weapons](/weapons/getting-started/). The official count is **25** (a few third-party databases count 26, likely due to Soul Temper transformation forms), split into five classes:

| Class | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Ranged** | Strike at distance, pick off casters |
| **Destruction** | Heavy damage and guard break |
| **Martial** | Direct combat techniques |
| **Artifice** | Machines and contraptions |
| **Summon** | Call allied force into the fight |

You equip **two at a time** and trigger them with **R2**, with a chargeable release for stronger effects.

![Soul unleashes a spectral edge technique, ink and embers spiraling around him](/images/articles/pbz-phantom-edges.jpg)

## What do Remnant Sparks do?

Both equipped Edges draw from a shared pool of **Remnant Spark (暗源) charges** — each use spends one, and resting at a Bell restores them. That makes Edges a managed resource: devastating when spent deliberately, gone when spammed. Many players hold charges for boss phase transitions, where the burst damage and guard break shine.

## Why are Edges also exploration tools?

Because they literally open the world. Edges are used to **shoot objects, break chains, burn brambles, and grapple across gaps** — a light Metroidvania layer where a new Edge picked up in one region unlocks shortcuts and caches in another. When you hit a bramble wall or a broken bridge, the answer is usually an Edge you don't have yet; note it and move on.

<Callout type="tip">Edges come from side quests, optional pickups, and Soul Tempering — so the [expiring purple quests](/guides/length-and-endings/) aren't just ending fodder; they're also arsenal fodder. New find? Test it on the nearest suspicious wall, and read the [weapons overview](/weapons/getting-started/) to plan your two equipped slots.</Callout>
