## What are Phantom Edges?

**Phantom Edges** are Soul's secondary armaments — you equip two and fire them with R2, spending **Remnant Sparks** per use. They come in five types, each with a distinct combat role and exploration function:

| Type | Combat role | Exploration use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Martial** | Martial-arts combos at various ranges | Grapple distant points ([Hunter's Chain Sickle](/bosses/the-hunt/)) |
| **Destructive** | Heavy hits that break guards | Break chains, barricades, weakened floors |
| **Ranged** | Bows and projectiles | Shoot distant objects and targets |
| **Artifice** | Devices with unique effects | Varies per Edge |
| **Summoning** | Allies that fight beside you | None established |

Many Edges double as **keys**: red obstacle icons on your map turn green once you hold the Edge that clears them — which is why backtracking with a new Edge is the game's core exploration loop (see the [map completion checklist](/guides/map-completion/)).

## Which Edges should I prioritize?

1. **Dread Axe first, always** — it is the valley's chain-barrier key and arrives from a mandatory-adjacent boss.
2. **Hunter's Chain Sickle second** — grapple access multiplies every region's reachable area.
3. **Flaming Mane** before exploring Pangzhen's blocked corners — burning brambles gates several Acolyte fights tied to the [true ending](/guides/true-ending/).
4. **Tiger Cannon** before the finale — its wall-blasting opens the last stretch of side content.

<Callout type="warn">The **Feather Bow** is the one Edge you can miss: its *Purge the Sect* tale reportedly fails once you push past the Crimson Forest main boss. Do the archer's quest the moment you reach the Outpost — missing it costs you a Ranged slot and the Phantom Arsenal trophy progress until your next cycle.</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">Each type upgrades with its own material, and levels under 10 refund for free — so when you adopt a new Edge type, plan a fresh material stockpile before committing dust (economy details in the [weapon upgrade guide](/weapons/weapon-upgrades/)).</Callout>
