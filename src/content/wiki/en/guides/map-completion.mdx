## What does map completion track?

Open the map screen and each region shows a **completion percentage** plus a checklist of what it still holds. The tracked categories:

| Category | What counts | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bells** | Rest/fast-travel points discovered | Healing, respec, upgrades, respawn anchors |
| **Weapons** | Main weapons found or dropped | Directly grows your arsenal — see the [tier list](/weapons/weapon-tier-list/) |
| **Phantom Edges** | Edges collected | Combat power **and** exploration keys ([all 25 locations](/weapons/phantom-edge-locations/)) |
| **Accessories** | Equippable passives found | Slot into your build via the [items overview](/items/getting-started/) |
| **Potions** | Medicine upgrades and variants | Longer fights, more healing per rest |
| **Challenge locations** | Banyan Trees, Long Pillars, acolyte scarecrows | Hearts for advanced abilities, true-ending pillars, masks |

Completion is not cosmetic: the permanent stat collectibles that gate your [ability tree](/guides/abilities-progression/) (Blackstones, Sha-Chi Ambers, Memory Flowers) sit inside these same chests and camps — sweeping the checklist **is** the leveling curve.

## Why sweep before you leave?

Three rules of this world punish "come back later":

1. **Enemies never respawn.** Nothing refills behind you; the region only gets emptier.
2. **Side quests expire.** Push the main story and [purple tales lock](/guides/expiring-side-quests/) — some hide Bells, Edges, and collectibles of their own.
3. **New Edges reopen old maps.** Barrier icons turn green as your Edge collection grows (the Dread Axe's chains, Flaming Mane's brambles, the Tiger Cannon's walls). Every new Edge is a reason to revisit earlier regions.

## What is the efficient sweep routine?

**On first entry — the light sweep (10–15 minutes):**
1. Light every Bell you pass; rest once to register the region checklist.
2. Follow the main road but peek down every side path — suspicious detours end in chests.
3. Read every note and wall inscription for [Remnant Dust](/guides/remnant-dust-farming/) and quest triggers.

**Before leaving the region — the exit sweep (20–30 minutes):**
1. Open the map; work the checklist top to bottom, nearest unchecked item first.
2. Clear camps guarding chests — camps are where fragments and accessories live.
3. Confirm no purple tale is still open in the journal.
4. Only then push the main quest.

**On gaining a new Edge — the return sweep:**
1. Scan the world map for icons your new Edge can clear.
2. Fast-travel through the region in Bell order; clear and collect.
3. Update the checklist; note what remains for endgame cleanup.

<Callout type="tip">The map screen is the single highest-ROI habit in the game: ten seconds of checking beats twenty minutes of wandering. Pair it with the [boss order and rewards list](/bosses/boss-order/) so you always know which fight unlocks which region's next pass.</Callout>

## What carries over if I miss things?

Missed checklist items are not lost forever — they are deferred. A New Cycle ([NG+](/guides/new-cycle/)) resets maps, quests, and collectibles while keeping your equipment and progression items, so a second run is a faster, stronger sweep. The exception is **missed expiring tales**, which stay locked for that playthrough and can gate the [true ending](/guides/true-ending/) — the one sweep item with real stakes.

<Callout type="warn">Do not chase 100% in Weeping Creek Valley before at least the Dread Axe and Chain Sickle — several entries are physically unreachable without their barrier-breaking and grapple functions. Sweep what you can, exit at a comfortable percentage, and let the return sweeps finish the job.</Callout>
