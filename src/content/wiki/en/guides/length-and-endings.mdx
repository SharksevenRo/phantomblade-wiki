## How long is it?

- **Main story only:** about 20–25 hours
- **Full playthrough with side content:** 25–40 hours — the director's own figure is 25–30, while reviewers landing 40–50 were typically sweeping side content or playing Hellwalker
- **Completionism:** a full clear is achievable within a single playthrough, roughly 30–40 hours

Those numbers fit the structure: nine hand-painted, seamlessly joined regions that are wide and branching but not an open world.

![Soul stands at a fork of ink-wash paths, one lit by lanterns, one drowned in mist](/images/articles/pbz-endings.jpg)

## How many endings are there?

**Four named endings.** The director's pre-launch "8 endings" comment shipped as Final Battle (the true ending), Long's Roar, Annihilation, and the 66 Days-exclusive Undying Grudge — full details in the [endings guide](/guides/endings-guide/). Endings are shaped primarily by **side-quest completion** — the game tracks how much of the world's threads you followed, not just which final choice you pick. That makes the mid-game, not the final menu, the real ending selector.

## How do I get the true ending?

Two habits decide it:

1. **Do purple-icon side quests promptly.** These quests expire as the story advances — miss the window and the quest is gone for that run.
2. **Defeat the two optional bosses: Zhu Yuan and Bi Feng.** Both are required alongside full side completion for the true ending.

<Callout type="warn">Because purple quests can expire, a "clean up before the final boss" plan can quietly cost you the true ending. Treat every purple icon as time-limited the moment it appears.</Callout>

## What happens when I die?

Lightly punished: you respawn at the last bell, and multi-phase bosses restart you at the phase where you died. Die in a boss's second phase and you begin again at that second phase. When you're ready for stakes, [New Cycle](/guides/new-cycle/) adds Hellwalker and the 66-death 66 Days mode. For the journey itself, start with the [beginner's guide](/guides/getting-started/).
