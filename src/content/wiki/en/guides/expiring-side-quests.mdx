## Can side quests really expire?

Yes. Phantom Blade Zero's main story advances in eight chapters, and **some side quests have a shelf life** — push the main quest too far and a tale closes for good. The design is deliberate: it makes the world feel like it moves without you, and it makes the [true ending](/guides/true-ending/) a test of thoroughness rather than skill.

The good news is the game is fair about it:

- **NPCs warn you.** When a quest is close to expiring, characters tell you directly that time is running out.
- **Your journal keeps the clue.** The quest log shows which tales are still open and which are in danger — check it every time you rest at a Bell.

![A wanderer studies a journal by lantern light in an ink-wash town](/images/articles/pbz-side-quests.jpg)

## Which quests should I prioritize?

The **purple-icon tales** in your journal are the important ones. They are the game's major side-story chains, and they matter twice over:

1. Completing **every** purple tale is a hard requirement for the true ending and its gold trophy, With Heart and Blood.
2. Two of them lead to the optional bosses **Zhu Yuan and Commander Bi Feng** — also required for the true ending.

Everything without a purple icon is flavor: worth doing for Remnant Dust, materials, and world-building, but never missable-critical.

## What is the safe way to play?

1. **Rest at a Bell before every main-story beat.** Open the journal; if a purple tale is open, do it now.
2. **Sweep each region before leaving.** The map screen shows per-region completion — Bells, weapons, Phantom Edges, accessories, potions, and challenge locations. Treat 100% as the exit ticket.
3. **Talk to NPCs twice.** Some tales only advance on a second conversation, and others begin from readable notes or wall writing.
4. **Fight every optional boss you meet.** The two true-ending bosses are reached through the purple chains, not hidden puzzles — playing thoroughly naturally finds them.

<Callout type="warn">There is no time limit pressuring you. Soul's 66 days is story framing on a first playthrough — you cannot "run out of time" by exploring. Rushing the main quest is the only way to lose content.</Callout>

## What happens if I miss something?

- **Missed purple tales** lock you out of the true ending on that run. The normal ending still plays out, and nothing else is punished.
- **Recovery path:** start a Cycle (New Game+). Cycles keep your progression and let you redo the tale chains — see [New Cycle explained](/guides/new-cycle/). New Game+ adds no new story, so nothing you missed is gone forever, just deferred.
- **Missed regular quests** cost you their dust and material rewards — annoying, not run-breaking. If you are optimizing the economy, pair this habit with the [weapon upgrade strategy](/weapons/weapon-upgrades/) so every region's finite supply lands where it counts.

<Callout type="tip">Completionists chasing the platinum in one playthrough should treat the journal like a checklist: purple tales first, region sweep second, main quest last. That single habit order covers every missable in the game.</Callout>
