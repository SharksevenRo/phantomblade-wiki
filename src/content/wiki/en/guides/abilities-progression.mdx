## Is Remnant Dust experience points?

No — **Remnant Dust is a currency, not XP**. You spend it to train abilities, upgrade weapons and Phantom Edges, and buy goods. There is no character level; your strength comes from three places: ability training, equipment upgrades, and permanent collectible bonuses. And since defeated enemies stay dead when you rest, dust and materials come from **exploring**, not farming — the world hands you a finite but generous supply.

![Soul meditates beneath a bell as golden dust drifts through the air](/images/articles/pbz-progression.jpg)

## How do abilities work?

You train abilities at any **Bell**. There are about **32 of them**, priced from free to 85,000 Remnant Dust, arranged in three rows. Each row is unlocked by collecting enough of a matching material — Black Stones, Sha-Chi Ambers, or Memory Blossoms (see below). Two comfort rules make experimenting safe:

- **Resets refund 100% of your dust.** Respec anytime, free of regret.
- Early nodes are cheap and immediately useful.

Strong early picks:

| Ability | Cost | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fluid Form | Free | Foundational mobility node |
| Healing Art | 5,000 | Adds an elixir charge — more forgiveness |
| Steady Composure | 5,000 | Steadier defense economy |
| Boundless Capacity | 10,000 | Extra accessory slot |
| Thousand-Mile Stride | 10,000 | Faster sprinting — exploration pays for itself |

## Which collectibles matter?

| Collectible | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Black Stone** (4 fragments combine) | Permanent +10% attack and guard break; unlocks skill-chain nodes |
| **Sha-Chi Amber** (4 fragments) | Permanent +10% maximum Sha-Chi |
| **Memory Blossom** (petal sets) | Permanent max-health increases (~5% / 7% / 10% tiers) |
| **Banyan Heart** | Late-game advanced material — roughly eight per playthrough, sold for 100,000 dust each by Quick Hand Zhang |

These are why detours matter: a fragment behind a side path is a permanent stat you carry to the final boss.

<Callout type="tip">Weapon upgrades and Phantom Edge upgrades also draw on dust and materials, and weapon upgrades unlock new skills at key thresholds. The full arsenal rules are in the [weapons guide](/weapons/getting-started/), and the currency flows into accessories too — see [items & resources](/items/getting-started/).</Callout>
