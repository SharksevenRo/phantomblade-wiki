## How do I train abilities?

Rest at any **Bell** and open **Train Abilities**. Every node costs **Remnant Dust** — the same currency you use for weapon upgrades and shopping — and most nodes also check how many upgrade collectibles you hold before they unlock (see the [collectibles guide](/guides/upgrade-collectibles/) for how that gating works). There is no XP bar in Phantom Blade Zero: the ability tree is the main way to make Soul stronger between weapon upgrades.

Two rules make early picks stress-free:

- **Resets refund 100% of your dust.** Respec at any Bell costs nothing, so a wrong pick is never permanent.
- **Enemies stay dead.** Dust is a finite regional supply, so spend it on tools you use in every fight, not niche tricks.

## What should I unlock with my first 30,000 Remnant Dust?

These five nodes are the cheapest useful picks on the tree, and together they cover mobility, healing, defense, build slots, and traversal:

| Ability | Cost | Effect | Why first |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fluid Form | Free | Quick Pursuit dash after counters and kills | The tutorial teaches it — it keeps pressure on groups |
| Healing Art | 5,000 | +1 Medicine use per rest | Medicine is your main heal between Bells |
| Steady Composure | 5,000 | Stronger interrupts after dodges and parries | Rewards learning the red/blue flash system |
| Boundless Capacity Lv. 1 | 10,000 | +1 accessory slot | Accessories carry some of the strongest passives |
| Thousand-Mile Stride | 10,000 | High-speed sprint after 2 seconds | Saves real time on every backtrack |

<Callout type="tip">Train **Healing Art** before long optional fights. Optional duels like Hua, Deadly Betrothed punish every missed dodge, and one extra elixir charge is often the difference between a clear and another run back from the Bell.</Callout>

## What should I unlock next?

Once the cheap tier is trained, the 20,000–40,000 nodes reward the core combat loop of swapping weapons, executing staggered enemies, and spending Phantom Edges:

- **Masterful Flow (30,000):** swapping weapons refills Sha-Chi instantly, but the swap has a cooldown — this shortens it by 2 seconds. Pair it with a two-weapon loadout from the [weapons guide](/weapons/getting-started/).
- **Endless Renewal (20,000):** assassinations and executions restore HP and Sha-Chi. Open camps with chained stealth kills, then execute Guard-Broken enemies to stay topped up.
- **Still Depths (30,000):** +1 maximum Remnant Spark, giving you one more Phantom Edge attack between rests — strong if your build leans on a heavy Edge like the Dread Axe (see [Phantom Edges](/weapons/phantom-edges/)).
- **Boundless Capacity Lv. 2 (40,000):** a fourth accessory slot, best bought once accessory buffing opens up later in the game.

## How do I respec if I pick wrong?

Respec anytime, free of regret:

1. Rest at any Bell — including fast travel points you unlocked earlier.
2. Open **Train Abilities** and select **Reset**.
3. The tree clears and your full Remnant Dust total returns.
4. Retrain your new picks — hold confirm on each node until it reads complete.

<Callout type="info">Weapon upgrades refund separately: reverting a weapon returns its materials and dust for free up to Level 10; higher levels need a Reforging Talisman. Plan your dust split between the ability tree and gear with the [upgrade economy guide](/weapons/weapon-upgrades/).</Callout>
