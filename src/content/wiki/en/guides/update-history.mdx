## What is the current version of Phantom Blade Zero?

The current live version is **1.0302256**, released **November 3, 2026** on WeGame and **November 4** on Steam and PlayStation 5. The download is roughly **3.7 GB**. This was the first update since launch — the game released on **October 29, 2026** for PC and PS5 and sold **four million copies in its first 72 hours**.

![A mechanical tower with glowing gears and freshly posted scroll notices](/images/articles/pbz-update-history.jpg)

## What does patch 1.0302256 change?

### New features

- **Hit Sound Intensity** and **Hit Impact Sound Intensity** options in audio settings — you can now tune how loudly hits and blocks call back during combat.
- **[PC]** **Interact / Talk can be remapped** on both keyboard and controller.

### Improvements and balance

- Performance improvements in certain areas.
- Adjusted combat behavior for certain bosses and enemies, and functionality of certain environmental mechanisms.
- **Hua, Deadly Betrothed now uses grab attacks less often on Wayfarer and Pathbreaker difficulties.** Hellwalker and 66 Days are unaffected — our [Hua boss guide](/bosses/hua-deadly-betrothed/) notes what this means for each difficulty.
- Improved weapon hit feedback; adjusted volume of certain sound effects and dialogue.
- Updated weapon descriptions, certain skill descriptions, and controller button icons.
- **[PC]** Shader compilation reworked: ray-tracing shaders no longer compile at startup by default, significantly cutting launch time. Players who had ray tracing off will recompile once on first launch after the patch (a short one-time process); enabling ray tracing later compiles the needed shaders on the next restart.
- **[PC]** Reduced stuttering with Bluetooth-connected controllers.
- **[PC]** NVIDIA Reflex now works when set to "Auto" with V-Sync enabled.

### Bug fixes

- Fixed issues that could block progression under certain conditions.
- Fixed environmental spots where the character could get stuck.
- Fixed cutscene and visual issues, several combat-related issues, and incorrect hair rendering on certain AMD graphics cards with specific driver versions.

<Callout type="info">S-GAME's patch note warns that some third-party mods are not compatible with the update — if problems appear after updating, remove installed mods first.</Callout>

## How do I check my version and update?

- **Steam:** the version builds automatically on launch; the build ID for 1.0302256 is 26367565 (visible in Properties → Updates or the Downloads page).
- **PS5:** check the version string on the game's dashboard tile options menu.
- **WeGame:** updates through the client; WeGame received the patch one day ahead of the global rollout.
