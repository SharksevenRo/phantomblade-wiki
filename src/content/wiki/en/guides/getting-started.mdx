## Who am I and what am I doing?

You play **Soul (魂)**, a master swordsman whose heart has been fatally wounded — he has **66 days left to live**. Rather than wait for death, Soul draws his blade against the conspiracy that destroyed him, cutting through assassins, monsters, and the strange machinery of a martial world in chaos. The story plays out across **nine hand-painted, ink-wash regions** joined seamlessly — semi-open maps with branching routes, neither a single corridor nor a true open world.

![Soul stands in the rain, blade drawn, beneath a lantern-lit rooftile skyline](/images/articles/pbz-getting-started.jpg)

## What are Bells?

**Bells are the backbone of the whole game.** Resting at one:

- Restores your health, elixirs, and Phantom Edge charges
- Acts as your respawn point and **fast-travel** node
- Opens the upgrade, store, and ability-training menus
- Reveals the nearby map the first time you touch it
- Offers an explicit **Save** option — you can hard-save your progress

Death sends you back to the last bell you touched. Defeated normal enemies **stay dead** when you rest, so the world slowly clears as you push forward.

<Callout type="tip">S-GAME has said plainly the game is <em>not</em> a soulslike. Blocking and poking is the slowest way to play — the [combat system](/guides/combat-system/) rewards constant offense, combos, and turning defense into counterattacks.</Callout>

## What should I do first?

1. Follow the opening until you unlock your first bell, then spend a minute reading its menu — everything important lives there.
2. Learn the color language of attacks early: blue can be parried, red must be dodged, dark attacks drain your Sha-Chi — the [combat guide](/guides/combat-system/) explains all of it.
3. Grab every collectible you pass. Black Stones, Sha-Chi Ambers, and Memory Blossoms are permanent power — see the [progression guide](/guides/abilities-progression/).
4. Talk to NPCs with **purple side-quest icons** promptly: those quests expire, and they feed the true ending (covered in [length & endings](/guides/length-and-endings/)).

## Do enemies respawn?

No — at least not from resting. Enemies you kill stay dead when you rest or respawn at a bell, so you can't farm the same pack for currency. Resources come from exploration, and the game is balanced around that. If a route feels rich, take the detour.

## How long is the game?

A straight main-story run runs about **20–25 hours**; a full playthrough with side content lands around 25–40, and completionists can finish nearly everything in a single run. Details and the four endings live in the [length & endings guide](/guides/length-and-endings/).
