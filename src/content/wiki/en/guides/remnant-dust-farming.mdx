## Can you farm Remnant Dust in Phantom Blade Zero?

**Not by respawning enemies — because enemies never respawn.** Resting at a Bell heals you and refills your potions, but the map stays cleared. There is no lantern-style reset loop and no repeatable grind spot. Dust — the currency that funds your entire [ability tree](/guides/best-abilities-first/), [weapon upgrades](/weapons/weapon-upgrades/), and shopping — is a **finite supply per playthrough**, padded by a handful of recurring sources.

So "farming" in Phantom Blade Zero really means **sweeping efficiently**: taking every dust source a region offers before you leave it for good.

![Golden dust motes drift through a dark bamboo forest](/images/articles/pbz-dust-farming.jpg)

## Where does Remnant Dust come from?

1. **Chests and guarded camps.** Every region hides chests, usually behind an enemy camp. Dust amounts scale with region depth — late-game caches hold far more than valley chests.
2. **Readable world text.** Notes, book pages, and writing on walls pay dust on first read and sometimes start [side quests](/guides/expiring-side-quests/) whose rewards include more. Read everything, once.
3. **Boss rewards.** Story and optional bosses both pay heavily; optional duels like [Hua, Deadly Betrothed](/bosses/hua-deadly-betrothed/) sit far above the story curve.
4. **Side quests and tales.** Purple-icon tales and smaller requests pay dust on completion — another reason to clear them before they expire.
5. **Refunds.** Reverting a weapon returns its dust and materials for free up to Level 10. "Farming" your own past mistakes is real income: a weapon you upgraded at level 10 and abandoned is dust sitting in storage.

## What is the most efficient sweep order?

Per region, in priority order:

1. **Follow the map checklist.** The map screen shows a completion percentage per region and lists what remains — Bells, weapons, Phantom Edges, accessories, potions, challenge locations. It is your farming route.
2. **Break every barrier you can.** Red obstacle icons turn green when you hold the matching [Phantom Edge](/weapons/phantom-edge-locations/) — the Dread Axe's chains, the War Wagon's barricades. New Edge, new sweep of old maps.
3. **Camp-check side paths.** If a path looks suspicious, it holds a chest. This heuristic rarely fails.
4. **Read before you rest.** Collect every note and inscription while passing through; do not plan a second trip, because the trip back costs playtime, not dust.

<Callout type="tip">The real economy decision is not where to farm — it is where to **spend**. With supply finite, split dust deliberately between abilities and weapons using the [upgrade economy guide](/weapons/weapon-upgrades/), and refund anything you have outgrown.</Callout>

## What about later playthroughs?

In a New Cycle ([NG+](/guides/new-cycle/)), quests, maps, and key items reset — which means **every dust source comes back**. A second run is the true "farm": your carried-over gear makes the sweep dramatically faster, and the [Cycle abilities](/guides/upgrade-collectibles/) convert what you collect into stacking passives.

<Callout type="warn">One habit to drop from other action RPGs: waiting to explore "when you are stronger." In Phantom Blade Zero, waiting usually means a [side quest expires](/guides/expiring-side-quests/) or you carry an underfunded build into the next boss. Sweep now; power follows.</Callout>
