## What is Sha-Chi?

**Sha-Chi (杀气)** is the yellow bar under your health — and it does three jobs at once:

- **Stamina:** holding guard and getting blocked drains it
- **Guard meter:** when *either* side's Sha-Chi empties, that side is Guard-Broken and open to a high-damage punish
- **Skill resource:** your Sha-Chi combos and special techniques spend it

Normal attacks and sprints are free. Everything defensive or flashy draws from the same pool, so running empty means losing both your offense and your guard.

## How do I read enemy attacks?

Enemy techniques come in three colors, and the color tells you the answer:

| Color | Meaning | Your answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Blue (Brutal Move)** | Heavy, parryable strike | **Parry** — a perfect parry costs nothing and triggers Ghoststep; turtling behind your guard bleeds Sha-Chi fast |
| **Red (Killer Move)** | Unblockable | **Dodge** — perfect dodging also triggers Ghoststep |
| **Dark/gray** | Sha-Chi-draining technique | **Disengage** — these drain or empty your Sha-Chi; most guides treat them as attacks you avoid rather than answer |

<Callout type="warn">Sources disagree on whether dark attacks can be blocked at a massive Sha-Chi cost. The safe rule: when you see the dark flash, create distance.</Callout>

## What is Ghoststep?

**Ghoststep** is the reward for perfect defense: Soul **teleports behind the attacker**, opening a backstab or combo window and refunding part of your Sha-Chi. Both a perfect parry (against blue) and a perfect dodge (against red) can trigger it. It converts a defensive read into your strongest offensive position.

## What are Power Surges and executions?

- **Power Surge (L2)** spends the Surges you build during combat for a high-damage strike that **ignores defense**. Its animation grants invulnerability.
- **Executions** on weakened or Guard-Broken enemies also give i-frames — and **refill your Sha-Chi**, making them part of your economy, not just a finisher.

## Why do I carry two weapons?

You equip **two main weapons** and swap between them instantly — and **swapping refills your Sha-Chi**, on a cooldown of roughly five seconds (skills can shorten it). When your bar runs dry mid-fight, the swap is your engine: refill, change your moveset, keep attacking. This is why S-GAME calls the design offense-first — the resource loop pushes you forward, not back.
