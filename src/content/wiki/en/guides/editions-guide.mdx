## What editions exist?

Two digital editions, on every storefront:

| | Standard | Digital Deluxe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US | $59.99 | $69.99 |
| China (all PC stores) | ¥268 | ¥328 |
| Hong Kong (PSN) | HK$328 | HK$398 |

Platforms at launch: **PS5, Steam, Epic Games Store, WeGame, and TapTap PC** — same content everywhere, and the install is hefty (roughly 145 GB on PC, 135 GB on PS5), so clear space before release night.

![A masked swordsman crosses a moonlit bridge between paper-lantern towns](/images/articles/pbz-editions.jpg)

## What do pre-order bonuses do?

Pre-ordering **either edition** early-unlocks two items:

- **Cornucopia accessory** — increases Remnant Dust drop rate by 10% while worn
- **Legacy outfit** — a cosmetic look for Soul

Digital Deluxe adds two more exclusive outfits — **Martial Legend** and **Mechanical Demon Body** — plus the digital artbook and soundtrack.

<Callout type="info">Crucially, every pre-order item — the accessory and all outfits — can also be unlocked through normal play. Buying early only gets you there sooner; nothing is permanently exclusive or grants a power advantage.</Callout>

## Which edition should I buy?

- **Standard** is the complete game. If you don't care about cosmetics, nothing gameplay-relevant is missing.
- **Deluxe** makes sense if you love the art direction (the artbook and OST are the real value) or want the two outfits without waiting.

If you're deciding between platforms rather than editions, note there's **no confirmed cross-progression** — pick where you'll finish the game.
