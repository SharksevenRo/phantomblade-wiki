## How many endings does Phantom Blade Zero have?

**Four named endings** in the release version: **Final Battle**, **Long's Roar**, **Annihilation**, and **Undying Grudge**. If you have seen "eight endings" quoted — including in some of our own early guides — that comes from director Soulframe Liang's pre-launch comments; no one has documented eight distinct finales, and launch-week guides consistently catalog **four** (see the [update history](/guides/update-history/) for how we track post-launch changes). Treat "8" as an aspiration that shipped as 4, plus ending-text variations.

![A swordsman faces two mountain paths, one gold and one dark red](/images/articles/pbz-endings.jpg)

## What triggers each ending?

Every ending except Undying Grudge resolves at **Mount Soulrend**, after the Leng Tu and Meng Hua fights. The game checks your completion the moment Meng Hua falls:

| Ending | Trigger | Trophy | Costume rewards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Final Battle** (true ending) | All true-ending requirements complete (checklist below) | **With Heart and Blood** (Gold) | The Hunt, Kindred, Blizzard appearances; Aureate weapon |
| **Long's Roar** | Everything done **except** the Long Pillar trials (Bloodline Awakening unfinished) | All Dreams Must End | Blizzard outfit |
| **Annihilation** | The lake secrets (or other requirements) unfinished — the default ending | All Dreams Must End (from the Meng Hua kill) | None |
| **Undying Grudge** | Your **66 Days** run counter hits zero | None | None — and Soul's story ends badly |

## What does the Final Battle (true ending) require?

The full checklist, consolidated:

1. **Complete every purple-icon Tale** in the journal — [they expire](/guides/expiring-side-quests/), so clear them as they appear.
2. **Finish Secrets at the Heart of the Lake**: the longest chain in the game, spanning multiple regions.
3. **Repair the Illusory Pipa** — collect its three parts (head from Dusk the Puppeteer, strings from Yan Qiansui, third part from Yubi), then have Myst repair it in the Pangzhen tavern.
4. **Collect all four Acolyte Masks** from the scarecrow acolyte fights (Yanru, Lei Ting, Guhe, Hansui).
5. **Obtain both Crystal Puppet Eyes** — Yin from the Mountain of Imprisonment, Yang from the Crimson Manor secret boss.
6. **Complete all five Long Pillar trials** (Bloodline Awakening) — the Gold-Masked One waits inside each.
7. **Defeat the two optional blade masters** — Zhu Yuan in the Pangzhen Sha-Chi Tower and Bi Feng in the Mountain of Imprisonment (see the [boss order list](/bosses/boss-order/)).
8. **Finish Quick Hand Zhang's requests** — 16 relics in glowing chests across five regions.

With everything done, defeat Leng Tu and Meng Hua; Zuo Shang then appears for the true final duel and its quick-time finale. The detailed walkthrough lives in our [true ending guide](/guides/true-ending/).

## What are the two points of no return?

1. **The Mirage Estate door.** Purple-icon tales lock the moment you enter the estate. Anything unfinished at that threshold is gone for this playthrough — only a save from before the door or a new cycle restores it.
2. **The Last Chance bell.** Past this bell, the final sequence begins; finish the fifth Long Pillar and Zhang's last relics (three sit in Mirage Estate) before resting here.

<Callout type="warn">After the credits, you can load back to **The Last Chance bell** to clean up anything still open post-estate. But a purple Tale that locked at the Mirage Estate door stays locked — that specific failure needs a pre-door save or a [New Cycle](/guides/new-cycle/).</Callout>

## What is the fastest route to all four endings?

1. **First run — finish everything except the fifth Long Pillar.** All purple Tales, both Eyes, four pillars, Zhu Yuan, Bi Feng, Zhang's relics — skip only the Mirage Estate pillar.
2. **Rest at The Last Chance bell and make a manual save.** Beat Leng Tu and Meng Hua for **Long's Roar**.
3. **Load the save, clear the fifth pillar, beat the finale again** for **Final Battle**.
4. **Start a New Cycle, run only gold main quests** to Meng Hua for **Annihilation** — carried-over weapons and Edges make this fast.
5. **Run a 66 Days save** and burn the counter for **Undying Grudge**.

<Callout type="tip">Final Battle is the ending to chase first: it is the only route where Soul survives on his own terms, and it pays out the Aureate weapon, three appearances, and the only ending-tied Gold trophy on the road to platinum.</Callout>
