## How many endings does Phantom Blade Zero have?

**Four named endings** shipped in the release version: **Final Battle** (the true ending), **Long's Roar**, **Annihilation**, and **Undying Grudge** — the director's pre-launch "8 endings" comment became four, plus ending-text variations (full breakdown in the [endings guide](/guides/endings-guide/)). Which one you reach depends on how much optional content you complete — side quests, key items, and the choices inside that side content all feed the branch. The main path alone resolves to **Annihilation**, which is not trophy-relevant. Final Battle is the only ending tied to a gold trophy: **With Heart and Blood**.

![Two warriors face each other on a snowy ledge at dusk](/images/articles/pbz-true-ending.jpg)

## What does the true ending require?

The launch-day trophy roadmap pins the exact requirements:

1. **Complete every tale marked with a purple icon in your journal.** These are the game's major side-story chains, and they appear progressively as the main story advances.
2. **Defeat the two optional bosses Zhu Yuan and Commander Bi Feng.** The same two fights also award the silver trophy The Two Blade Masters, so you knock out two goals at once.

<Callout type="warn">Some side quests **expire as the main story moves on**. NPCs warn you before a quest locks, and your journal keeps the clue — but the rule stands: take every purple tale the moment it appears, and clear it before you push the main quest forward. A full walkthrough of the timing lives in the [expiring side quests guide](/guides/expiring-side-quests/).</Callout>

## Does difficulty or cycle matter?

No. The true ending is about **completion, not challenge**:

- It is reachable on any difficulty — Wayfarer, Pathbreaker, or Hellwalker (compared in the [difficulty modes guide](/guides/difficulty-modes/)).
- The trophy roadmap confirms the platinum is achievable in a single playthrough, so you do not need New Game+ cycles for the ending itself.
- **New Game+ adds no new story** — if you missed the true ending on your first run, a Cycle run (see [New Cycle explained](/guides/new-cycle/)) is the cleanest way to chase the remaining endings and the collectible checks behind them.

## How do I avoid missing the true ending?

- **Check the journal every time you rest at a Bell.** Purple icons are your to-do list for the true ending; everything else is optional flavor.
- **Do not leave a region with open purple tales.** Story progression can quietly close them.
- **Fight every optional boss you find.** Zhu Yuan and Bi Feng are not hidden behind obscure puzzles — they are proper side encounters you meet through the purple tale chains, so playing thoroughly naturally leads to both.
- **Talk to NPCs twice.** Some tales only advance after a second conversation, and a few start from notes or wall writing that reward Remnant Dust too.

<Callout type="tip">Soul's 66 days is **story framing only** on a first playthrough — there is no time limit pressuring you to rush. Take the detours; the [length and endings overview](/guides/length-and-endings/) breaks down what a completionist run actually takes.</Callout>
