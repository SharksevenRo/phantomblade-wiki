## Why do collectibles matter more than levels?

Phantom Blade Zero has no character level. Permanent power comes from **collectibles**, and each one you grab is a stat increase you keep for the entire journey — including into New Game+ cycles. Four items feed the whole system:

| Collectible | Found as | Permanent effect | Abilities unlocked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Blackstone** | 4 fragments combine into 1 | +10% Attack and Guard Break | New nodes at 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 stones |
| **Sha-Chi Amber** | 4 fragments combine into 1 | +10% maximum Sha-Chi | New nodes at 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 ambers |
| **Memory Flower** | Petal sets form a large flower | +10% maximum Health per large flower | New nodes per completed set |
| **Banyan's Heart** | Whole item, 8 per playthrough | Unlocks the 8 advanced inter-row abilities | One heart per node |

The ability rows are gated by these counts: a node that asks for 4 Blackstones means **16 fragments** collected in total. High-end nodes therefore only open near the end of the campaign, after you have swept nearly every corner — which is why thorough exploration is the real difficulty setting.

## Where do I find fragments and flowers?

- **Guarded chests.** Fragments sit in chests scattered across the nine hand-painted regions, usually defended by an enemy camp. If a side path looks suspicious, it almost always pays.
- **Rare whole stones.** Occasionally a complete Blackstone or Sha-Chi Amber is found instead of fragments — a four-fragment shortcut.
- **Map completion tracking.** The map screen shows a completion percentage per region plus a checklist of what it still holds: Bells, weapons, Phantom Edges, accessories, potions, and challenge locations. Use it to sweep a region before moving on.
- **Banyan Tree challenges.** The eight **Banyan's Hearts** drop from Banyan Tree encounters — wave fights that escalate into duo boss battles. Details in the [Banyan Tree challenges guide](/bosses/banyan-tree-challenges/).

<Callout type="tip">Enemies stay dead when you rest, so there is no farming loop — the world gives you a finite supply of everything. Every fragment you skip is a permanent stat you never get back on that run.</Callout>

## What are Banyan's Hearts used for?

The second and fourth rows of the ability tree are **interconnected perks** that bridge the three main rows. Each activates only when its connected nodes are unlocked **and** you spend one Banyan's Heart:

| Ability | Dust cost | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Still Mind | 25,000 | Brief invincibility during a Power Surge |
| Flow Like Water | 12,500 | Blocking and dodging no longer interrupt your combo |
| Opening Strike | 35,000 | Weapon-swap move becomes a special attack |
| Flash Step | 45,000 | L1/R1 spend a little Sha-Chi for an instant special dodge |
| Quick Remedy | 45,000 | Using Medicine while being attacked cancels the stagger |
| Boundless Capacity Lv. 3 | 65,000 | +1 accessory slot |
| Cicada's Escape | 65,000 | When Guard Broken, auto-retreat with brief invulnerability |
| Against All Odds | 85,000 | Once per battle, survive one hit that would drop you below 10% HP |

With only **8 hearts per playthrough**, you cannot max everything at once — pick for your build. Most players shortlist **Flow Like Water**, **Quick Remedy**, and **Against All Odds** first.

## What about Cycle abilities?

After your first clear, a final tier of abilities opens up in New Game+ (Cycles). Each requires **8 of each collectible set plus a Banyan's Heart**, grants a stacking passive, and takes up to **5 ranks per Cycle (max NG+6)** — a long-tail progression path for repeat runs, explained in [New Cycle](/guides/new-cycle/).

<Callout type="info">Spending priority when dust is tight: weapon upgrades unlock skills at key thresholds too, so split materials deliberately — the [weapon upgrade economy guide](/weapons/weapon-upgrades/) shows the refund rules that make experimenting safe.</Callout>
